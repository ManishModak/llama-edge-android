## 2026-08-08 — Phase-aware `pp8-tg2` passes the frozen-device gate
- **Hypothesis:** topology-derived wide prefill with a narrower decode pool can preserve prompt
  processing while reducing bandwidth contention during token generation on MT6855.
- **Change / config:** Three-round counterbalanced `llama-bench` sweep at context 512 selected
  prefill 8 / decode 2 against stock 8 / 8. The same runtime switch was then exercised through the
  Android app with five measured repetitions per mode and three no-cooldown sustained suites.
- **Commits:** app parent `54aabbde5b9c` plus the fingerprinted worktree; llama.cpp `178a6c449371`
  plus source-diff fingerprint. Exact binary, model, policy, device, workload, and scoring hashes
  are retained in the raw artifacts.
- **Result:** The final autotune report passed all six gates. Across the sustained app evidence
  (15 runs/mode), baseline decode was `5.3897 +/- 0.8677 tok/s` and optimized decode was
  `11.1777 +/- 0.1908 tok/s` (`2.0739x`). Mean end-to-end duration fell from 25,244.9 ms to
  12,000.5 ms. All output-hash pairs matched. The session spanned 14.37 minutes and battery
  temperature rose from 35.5 C to 38.7 C without a low-memory signal.
- **Conclusion / next:** Accept only for the exact frozen identity; generic speedup claims remain
  prohibited. Add one short supplemental app export with the newly implemented VmHWM/SwapFree and
  source/JNI hashes, rebuild final APKs, then complete video/public-release/submission work.
- **Evidence:** `benchmarks/results/raw/20260807-232905-autotune/autotune.json` (SHA-256
  `7afd9ae000d57bf1bce0e3d38de13d99ffd779a34bbb591f9e41dabffc56a7ab`) and
  `benchmarks/results/20260807-real-generation/summary.json` (SHA-256
  `b2f447f615d51fa4c87084c17b83de78a271685d3ab21f7fe009d40f379c269f`).

## 2026-07-27 — N-gram fallback fails, and its speedup is a measurement artifact
- **Hypothesis:** ngram-mod on Llama 3.2 1B Q4_0 clears the 1.03x promotion gate without adding
  weights.
- **Change / config:** `tools/run_ngram_ab.py` on `phase3-ngram.json` as prepared — 2 modes × 3
  prompts × 5 repetitions, `-c 512`, t=6/tb=8, greedy, seed 42, 120 s inter-mode cooldown.
- **Commits:** app current worktree; llama.cpp `178a6c4`.
- **Result:** **Fail.** Verdict `fail` in
  `benchmarks/results/phase3-feasibility/20260727-204314-phase3-ngram/ngram-ab.json`. Failed gates:
  `rssIncreaseWithinLimit` (+139,780 KB vs 65,536 limit) and `noSwapUse` (`SwapFree` −91,392 KB vs
  65,536 limit). Thermal was clean (+0.2 °C) and all 15 output pairs were byte-identical.
  Recorded decode speedup was 2.97x (29.28 vs 9.86 tok/s) — **do not report it.**
- **Why the speedup is not real:** acceptance was 1076/1076 = **100.0 %**; repetition 1 measured
  ~9.7–9.9 tok/s on all three prompts (level with baseline) while repetitions 2–5 measured ~31–35;
  and the runner started **one** `llama-server` for all 15 runs. The n-gram cache therefore
  persisted across requests, and greedy decoding at a fixed seed made repetitions 2–5 regenerate
  text already resident in that cache. The drafter was replaying its own prior output.
  `repetitive-sequence` — the prompt designed to favour n-gram — gained nothing on first exposure
  (9.94 tok/s), which is the honest signal and matches the Phase 2 DRAM ceiling.
- **Conclusion / next:** Workstream D closed; both speculative candidates refuted with evidence.
  Gate G2 = No. Suite defect to fix before anyone re-runs this: cold-start the server per
  repetition, or vary the prompt per repetition. Pivot to Phase 3B (portable core-policy
  autotuner) per `docs/PLAN.md`.

## 2026-07-26 — Phase 3 native MTP does not promote
- **Hypothesis:** A 2.19 GB Gemma 4 E2B QAT `UD-Q2_K_XL` target plus its 59.2 MB
  smart-Q4_0 MTP drafter may fit without ZRAM and reduce target bytes streamed
  per emitted token enough to beat the Phase 2 DRAM ceiling.
- **Change / config:** Ran the fixed three-prompt, one-repetition
  `llama-server` feasibility A/B at `-c 512`: identical target and greedy request settings,
  `--spec-type none` versus explicit `--model-draft` plus
  `--spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 4`. This is the corrected rerun:
  the stream parser preserves both `reasoning_content` and visible `content` in
  wire order, so the non-empty-output and exact-output gates cover every
  generated byte.
- **Commits:** app `54aabbd` plus current worktree; llama.cpp `178a6c4` plus
  `patches/llama.cpp/0001-fix-speculative-draft-model-path.patch`. The pinned
  loader passed the target path where the explicit draft path was required;
  the private-checkout candidate patch corrects that one argument. The runner
  fingerprints the source diff and on-device binary in every evidence file.
- **Result:** **Fail.** Baseline decode was 4.91956 tok/s; MTP was 4.82293
  tok/s, or **0.98036x**. It accepted 226 of 605 drafted tokens (**37.36%**).
  All three outputs were non-empty and byte-identical. `SwapFree` fell by
  450,620 KB during baseline and 150,028 KB during MTP; peak process VmHWM was
  2,027,504 KB and 1,902,124 KB respectively. The device exported no usable
  ZRAM counter, so that metric is unknown rather than zero. Artifact:
  `benchmarks/results/phase3-feasibility/20260726-210042-phase3-mtp/mtp-ab.json`
  (SHA-256
  `81e92389437bfe9b02ab7c0b9d5dd41a29f5308bfbbd4711692b4ff114e58932`).
- **Conclusion / next:** Do not promote native MTP on this device. The drafter
  verified output correctly but did not recover its overhead at the observed
  acceptance. Move to the zero-weight ngram-mod fallback on Llama 1B.

## 2026-07-26 — Zero-weight ngram-mod fallback prepared
- **Hypothesis:** ngram-mod can reduce full-target decode steps on repetitive
  and code continuations without adding model weights or worsening the
  device's memory-bandwidth pressure.
- **Change / config:** Generalized the server A/B runner without changing the
  MTP path. The new `phase3-ngram.json` suite compares Llama 3.2 1B Q4_0 with
  `--spec-type none` against `ngram-mod` at match/min/max `24/4/32`, `-c 512`,
  six decode threads, eight batch threads, three fixed prompts, and five
  repetitions. Modes remain explicitly named `baseline` and `ngram`.
- **Commits:** app current worktree; llama.cpp `178a6c4`.
- **Result:** Harness, gates, and dry-run evidence validated only; no device run
  was performed while preparing the fallback.
- **Conclusion / next:** Run `python tools/run_ngram_ab.py --out-dir
  benchmarks/results/phase3-feasibility`. Promote only if outputs are non-empty
  and exact, drafting is active, mean decode is at least 1.03x, swap/RSS stay
  within suite limits, and battery temperature remains within the configured
  ceiling.
